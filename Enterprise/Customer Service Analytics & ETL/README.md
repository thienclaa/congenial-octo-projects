# Customer Service Analytics Platform

Enterprise operational analytics platform designed to monitor customer service performance, SLA compliance, and agent productivity across omnichannel support operations.

## Overview

Designed and maintained a Power BI analytics platform to monitor customer service operations across multiple teams.

The platform centralizes chat sessions, SLA metrics, response times, and operational KPIs into reusable semantic models and interactive dashboards, enabling daily operational monitoring and management reporting.

## Business Problem

The customer service department required a centralized reporting solution to monitor SLA compliance, agent performance, and operational efficiency.

Existing reports were fragmented, making it difficult to identify SLA breaches and monitor team performance in real time.

## Solution

Built an enterprise analytics platform integrating customer chat logs and operational data into Power BI dashboards.

The platform provides:

- SLA monitoring
- First response time analytics
- Assignment time monitoring
- Agent performance analytics
- Team performance dashboards
- Daily operational KPI reporting
